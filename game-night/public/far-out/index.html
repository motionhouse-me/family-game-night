How to play

Everyone gets the same 7 prompts. You have 25 seconds each. Any correct answer counts — but the rarer it is, the farther you fly.

🛰️ Solo flight +20 if nobody else in the room gave the same answer. Say the same thing as your sister? Jinx — no bonus.

🚨 No Googling. Leave the game during a question (switch apps, open a new tab) and that answer scores 0. Three strikes and you're ejected from the round. Pasting is blocked too.
